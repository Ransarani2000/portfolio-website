About

Low-cost hardware, rigorous engineering.

I'm a mechatronics undergraduate focused on robotics, ROS/ROS 2 systems, embedded hardware and mechanical design. I build autonomous platforms end to end, from PCB design and firmware to SLAM and navigation.

My final-year research delivered a LiDAR-based autonomous mobile robot for under LKR 120,000, including a custom isolation PCB that solved motor-induced processor crashes and a documented fallback after both wheel encoders failed.
